# Notice — what is not published and why

- **No media.** The source clip is the owner's own published video; renders,
  extracted frames, keyframes and the final clip are derivative works of a
  commercial music track. Publishing audio/video here would redistribute the
  music label's recording, so only text artifacts, code, metrics and telemetry
  are included.
- **No model weights.** LTX-2.5 and companion checkpoints are governed by
  their upstream licenses; fetch them from the sources named in
  `docs/HARDWARE_POWER.md` / `logs/models_dl.log`.
- **Redactions are minimal and mechanical:** account usernames/paths →
  placeholders, LAN addresses → none present after sanitization. Numbers,
  metrics, seed values, timings and error texts are unmodified.
- Personal messages (chat instructions) appear only as English translations
  in `docs/USER_FEEDBACK.md`, profanity removed.
